About GeoPad

GeoPad is a custom handheld project created with a focus on precision input and open hardware design. It is especially optimized for Geometry Dash players who want tighter control, better timing accuracy, and a physical device that matches fast rhythm gameplay.

The goal is to merge hardware tinkering with gaming performance, allowing players and makers to build, modify, and refine their own input experience.
